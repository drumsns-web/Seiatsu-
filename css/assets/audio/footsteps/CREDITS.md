# Footstep audio
Kenney, Impact Sounds 1.0 (2019), CC0.
Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
License verified 2026-10-30. Original license text is in License.txt.

Sources: footstep_concrete, footstep_wood, footstep_carpet, footstep_grass, variants 000-003.
Metal footsteps mix the concrete recordings with impactMetal_light_000-003 at 28% level.
Edits: mono conversion, resample to 22050 Hz, DC removal, peak normalization, short fades.
Processed WAV files are duplicated as embedded PCM in js/footstep-data.js for offline loading.
