The recordings are distinct sources; no pitch shifting or synthesized staple/ratchet sounds are used. Excerpts are faded, DC-corrected, bounded in peak level, and filtered/gain-damped by the game's actual contact surface. Metal/wood/carpet/grass versions are surface approximations made from the hard-floor recordings, not separately recorded takes on every floor. Later bounces are driven by the existing game physics, not a repeated full drop recording.

No source was purchased or uploaded to an external service. Source MP3 files remain unmodified. Speaker audition by the assistant has not been performed; numerical signal and lifecycle tests do not establish perceived naturalness.
